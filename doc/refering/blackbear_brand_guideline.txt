Logo
The Formosan Black Bear is the God of Mountains for
Taiwanese indigenous peoples. Without bears, the Bunun hunters say,
the forest is an empty dwelling. Formosan Black Bears are solid yet agile,
moving across large areas to find available resources from the wild.
Likewise, BlackBear companies are professional, experienced but flexible,
valuing innovation and ethics above all. We source and engineer efficient
solutions tailored for each customer.
